Assistant Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Assistant Bot handles information provided by users when they interact with the bot.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how Assistant Bot is configured, the bot may receive information such as a Discord username, Discord user ID, employee or staff number, and information related to an account-support request.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to verify a user's identity, process account support requests, troubleshoot account-access problems, and improve the operation of Assistant Bot.

3. Passwords and Authentication Information

Assistant Bot should not request or permanently store users' passwords, one-time authentication codes, or other sensitive authentication credentials.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell or share user information with third parties except where necessary to operate the service, comply with legal requirements, or protect the security of the service.

5. Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, or meet applicable legal requirements.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by Assistant Bot against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Children's Privacy

Assistant Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data handling practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the Assistant Bot administrator.
